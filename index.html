Privacy Policy for Remember

1 Overview & Core Philosophy

Remember is an educational workflow and procedure-learning Android application created by Sonal Pal. It empowers users to capture, document, organize, and step-by-step replay learning procedures, study guides, and workflows.

We believe your personal study notes and learning workflows belong entirely to you. Our architecture is designed with an offline-first approach, prioritizing on-device data storage and local operations.

2 Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Remember, we may collect the following types of information:

  • User-Generated Study & Process Data: Information you voluntarily create within the app, such as process titles, step descriptions, explanations, educational notes, study categories, and creation timestamps.
  • Authentication & Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in to save or synchronize your processes across devices, we collect your email address and generated unique user identifier (UID) via Firebase Authentication.
  • Technical & Connectivity Diagnostics: Basic network status indicators to verify if your device has internet access before initiating cloud synchronization.

🔒 No Unnecessary Tracking: Remember does not collect your physical location, contacts, phone calls, SMS, microphone recordings, or biometric data.

3 Android Permissions Used

Remember requests only minimal, necessary system permissions to function reliably on your device:

Permission Type Purpose
android.permission.INTERNET Normal / Install-time Allows optional cloud account login and database sync with secure Firebase servers.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal / Install-time Enables the application to detect whether an active internet connection is available before attempting cloud sync.

4 Data Storage & Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your learning data:

  • Local On-Device Database: Your processes and steps are stored in an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite Room Database (AppDatabase) within the application's private storage directory. No other app on your device can access this data.
  • Encryption in Transit: Whenever data is transmitted between the app and backend services (such as Firebase Authentication), all communications are strictly encrypted using standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Cloud backend services are managed using Google Firebase infrastructure with role-based access rules.

5 Third-Party Services & SDKs

Remember utilizes verified, trusted third-party service providers compliant with Google Play Policies:

  • Google Play Services: Core application maintenance, runtime framework, and app delivery.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Database): Manages optional user account identity and encrypted cloud data storage.
    Firebase Privacy and Security Documentation

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or user-generated workflows with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

6 Your Data Rights & Deletion

In compliance with global data privacy regulations (including GDPR and Google Play User Data policies), you have complete control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete any process, guide, or step directly within the Remember app at any time.
  • Complete Account & Data Removal Request: You can request full deletion of your account and any associated cloud data by emailing the developer at palsonal57@gmail.com with the subject line "Account / Data Deletion Request - Remember". Requests are processed within 30 days.
  • App Uninstall: Uninstalling the application immediately wipes all locally stored offline database records from your device.

7 Children's Privacy

Remember is designed for general audiences and educational purposes. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions).

If you are a parent or guardian and discover that your child has provided us with personal information without consent, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove the information.
